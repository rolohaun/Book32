LILYGO · Ink Boy

Your game library

Game Boy, Game Boy Color and NES — in grayscale on e-paper.

Upload a game

Upload your legally obtained ROM to /roms on the MicroSD card. Ink Boy sorts it into a Game Boy or NES tab. Existing files are never replaced.

GB/GBC: up to 8 MB · NES (iNES): up to 2 MB. ZIP archives and NES 2.0 are not supported. No game audio.

Your games

Open Ink Boy on the LILYGO after uploading. Wi-Fi is paused while that app is open; return to the home screen to upload more.

InkDeck is open-source software. Game Boy/GBC and NES support save slots and automatic resume. Complete source and license notices.
